# Hints

<details><summary>Hint 1: how many rows</summary>

Three conditions with two values each: 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 rows. Pick ₦2,000 for "up to ₦5,000" and ₦8,000 for "over".

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 2: who to log in as</summary>

The tier comes from the account. Log in as Ada for Plus rows and as Tunde for Basic rows; the starter's `logInAs` does that for you.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 3: the suspicious corner</summary>

Every row with only one "special" condition passes. Try the rows where tier is Plus **and** express is on.

</details>
